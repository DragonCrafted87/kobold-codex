# Orchestration playbooks: large audit, large migration, cross-checked research

A revision of `run-the-play` and three new playbooks, from the
2026-11-09 multi-agent orchestration research. The wording below is
the version to ship.

## Why

Heavy parallel work is a composition of existing skills, not a new
orchestrator skill. Playbooks name when to load `fan-out`,
`loop-to-the-goal`, `isolate-the-work`, `map-the-handoffs`,
`keep-the-findings`, and `carry-out-the-plan`, and they remind the
session that tracker paths are OPTIONAL.

## Playbooks

The bodies of `large-audit.md`, `large-migration.md`, and
`cross-checked-research.md` beside `run-the-play` are the wording to
ship.

## Skill: run-the-play

The match gains the three task kinds above. The sentence on plugin
playbooks names the three files beside the basic four.

## What this change does not do

It does not add an orchestrator skill. It does not require a
tracker. It does not add a bounds key for worker caps. It names no
project, host, or tool.
